23.2Why our petitions are on our own website. Our petitions are run by ExecProsec on our own website, not on UK Parliament’s petitions website, and we decide when, how and to whom a petition is presented. We have chosen this deliberately. In our view, Parliament’s petition system is designed in a way that makes it harder for campaigns to succeed: it does not allow campaigners to email the people who signed, for example to ask them to share the petition, to contact their representatives or to come to an event or demonstration, which limits the momentum a campaign can build. In our experience, the responses that Parliament and the Government give to official petitions are often poorly researched and poorly worded and contain mistakes, and working directly with members of the House of Commons and the House of Lords is a more effective way to secure a debate. Our amendments have already been debated in both Houses, and we are working for a vote on them in the House of Lords. The purpose of our petitions is to measure the weight of public support, to gather the public’s own words, and to tell journalists and politicians how many people want the change.
