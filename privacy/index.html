Privacy policy

This policy explains how ExecProsec Limited collects, uses, shares and protects personal data across our campaign: our websites, donations, emails, events, social media accounts and the tools we use to run them. Last updated 18 October 2026.

1. Who we are

ExecProsec Limited (company number 10520032, registered in England and Wales; registered office Suite 1, First Floor, 3 Jubilee Way, Faversham, Kent ME13 8GD) is the controller of the personal data described here. "We", "us" and "our" mean ExecProsec Limited. Our founder is Marcus J Ball, and this policy also covers the social media accounts he uses for the campaign. Contact: mjb@execprosec.com.

2. Who this covers

Supporters and backers; people who give, sign up, write to us or attend our events; visitors to execprosec.org and our other sites; people who follow, comment on, reply to or mention our social media accounts or Marcus J Ball's; and people in public life whose public roles relate to our work, such as Members of Parliament, peers, officials, lawyers and journalists.

3. What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, postal address, phone number, social media handle, and anything else you give us.
  • Giving: the amounts, dates, frequency and method of your donations and subscriptions, and your payment status. Card and bank details are taken and held by our payment provider, Stripe, not by us.
  • Communications: the full content of emails, messages, forms, replies and comments you send us, and our replies.
  • Website and email use: pages viewed, links clicked, videos watched and for how long, whether emails are opened, personal links we have sent you, your device, browser and approximate location from your internet address.
  • Social media: what you publish to or about us or Marcus J Ball (your name, handle, public profile details, the words, pictures and reactions you post), received from the platforms.
  • Public sources: information that is already public, such as Parliament's records, Hansard, registers of interests, Companies House, court records, published statements and press coverage.
  • Views and quotes: what you tell us about our work and why you support it, which we may quote with your permission.

Supporting a campaign about the law and politics may reveal your political opinions, which the law treats as special category data. We handle it only where you have made it public yourself, where you have consented, or where the law otherwise allows.

4. How we use it

  • to run our campaign for the law, including our work in Parliament, the courts and the media;
  • to take, process and manage donations and subscriptions, and to keep financial records;
  • to keep you informed about our work and to ask for your support, including personalised updates and appeals based on your history with us (for example how long and how much you have given);
  • to answer your messages, comments and questions;
  • to publish to our social media accounts and to read, answer and moderate what is said in response;
  • to understand public discussion of our work and of the issues we campaign on;
  • to organise events, briefings and meetings;
  • for research, writing, journalism and documentary film-making about our work and its subject;
  • to quote supporters, with their permission;
  • to analyse and improve our websites, emails and campaigning;
  • to keep our systems secure and to prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to comply with the law and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

5. Software and artificial intelligence

We use software, including artificial intelligence assistants (for example Anthropic's Claude), to read, sort, summarise and draft replies to messages, comments and posts, to prepare emails and social media posts, and to analyse support for our work. Some replies are sent automatically, using answers we have written and approved. We do not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you by automated means alone.

6. Our legal grounds

  • Contract: to process your donations and subscriptions.
  • Legitimate interests: to campaign, fundraise, communicate with supporters, answer people who contact us, publish and moderate social media, research, analyse and improve our work, and keep our systems secure. We balance these against your rights, and you can object at any time.
  • Consent: where the law requires it, for example for some marketing emails and for quoting you. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial and tax records and to respond to lawful requests.
  • Legal claims: to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

7. Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only as needed with:

  • service providers who process it for us under contract, including Stripe (payments), Amazon Web Services (email delivery), Cloudflare (websites and video), Railway (hosting), Google (email and documents), Anthropic (AI assistance) and the social media platforms we use;
  • our professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants;
  • companies under common control with us, such as Radiator Productions Limited, where needed for our film and media work;
  • courts, regulators, the police or other authorities where the law requires or permits it, or to protect our rights or anyone's safety;
  • a successor organisation, if our work is transferred, under the same protections.

Some of these providers are in the United States or elsewhere outside the UK. Where data leaves the UK we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK-US data bridge) or approved contractual safeguards.

8. How long we keep it

Donation and financial records are kept for at least six years, as the law requires. Other data is kept for as long as it is useful for our campaign and our relationship with you, and as long as needed for legal purposes. We review what we hold and delete what we no longer need.

9. Emails

Every campaign email has an unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe or ask us to stop, we keep a short record of your address so that we do not email you again.

10. Cookies and similar technology

Our websites use your browser's storage to remember things such as where you are in our story, and record pages viewed and videos watched, including through personal links we send you. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our websites load fonts from Google and payment forms from Stripe, which receive your internet address when they do.

11. Social media

When you interact with our accounts or Marcus J Ball's on Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn or other platforms, those platforms share with us what you have made available to us, under their own privacy policies. We use it as described above, including to reply to you and to understand public discussion of our work.

12. Having your data deleted

Email mjb@execprosec.com with the subject "Delete my data", saying which of our services you used and, for social media, your username on the platform (for example @yourname). We will delete what we hold about you within one month and confirm when it is done, except anything we must keep by law or need for a legal claim, which we will tell you about. Deleting a comment on a platform does not by itself remove our copy, so please write to us as well.

13. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, and for it to be corrected, deleted, restricted or transferred. You can object to our use of it based on legitimate interests, and you can always object to direct marketing. Contact us at the address above; we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

14. Children

Our campaign is aimed at adults. We do not knowingly take donations from, or collect data about, anyone under 18.

15. Security, other websites and changes

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, but no system is completely secure. Our websites and messages link to other sites, which have their own policies and for which we are not responsible. We may update this policy at any time; the current version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated. Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have by law.
