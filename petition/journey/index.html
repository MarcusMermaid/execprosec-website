Up to two years in prison for public officials proven to have intentionally or recklessly deceived the public. It’s in the Public Office (Accountability) Bill, known as the Hillsborough Law.

We want to end the carve-out for every lawmaker in the UK.

This is not a polite petition. It’s an email campaigning machine.

  • Our amendment is written.
  • It has been debated in the House of Lords.
  • Peers from across the House of Lords back our call.
  • Next: one big vote in the House of Lords, expected in December.
  • If we win, a vote in the House of Commons.
