Petition to Parliament & Press

Include all lawmakers under the Hillsborough Law

For the first time, UK law will make it a crime for people in public office to deliberately or recklessly mislead the public. The Hillsborough Law will cover police officers, civil servants, the armed forces, NHS doctors and nurses, Government ministers, the Prime Minister and even the intelligence services. But the lawmakers writing it have left themselves out.

This petition, which we will present to Parliament and the press, calls on every lawmaker in the UK, including MPs, peers, and members of the Scottish Parliament, the Senedd and the Northern Ireland Assembly, to lead by example and include themselves. Anything less would be hypocritical, a betrayal of everything the Hillsborough Law stands for, an affront to equality under the law, and potentially the most damaging blow to public trust in our democracy the UK has ever seen.
