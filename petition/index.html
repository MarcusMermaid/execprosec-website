Petition to Parliament

Include all politicians in the Hillsborough Law

We call on Parliament to include every MP, every peer and every other legislator in the UK in the Hillsborough Law's offence of misleading the public, for what they say and do outside Parliament.

The new law makes it a crime for public officials, including Government ministers, to mislead the public. MPs and peers are left out.

    Why this matters

    The Public Office (Accountability) Bill, known as the Hillsborough Law, is going through Parliament now. It creates a duty of candour and a new crime of misleading the public.

    It covers public officials, including Government ministers. MPs and peers are left out.

    For months the Government said they had to be left out because Parliament deals with its own members. On 21 October it accepted in the House of Lords that nothing said in Parliament could be prosecuted under this law anyway, because of parliamentary privilege.

    If an MP or a peer deliberately lies to the public outside Parliament, on television, on the radio, at a public event or on social media, they should face the same law as everyone else in public office.

    Regardless of which party is in power.

    What happens next

    1. Peers vote at Report stage. The House of Lords votes on bringing MPs and peers into the law.
    2. We hand it in. Every signature, with the most seconded quotes, goes to Parliament and to journalists before the vote.
    3. We make it impossible to ignore. With your help, the quotes go on newspaper pages, the Tube and the radio, and we stand outside Parliament on the day.
