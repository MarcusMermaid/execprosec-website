      How these figures are made

      Only people who signed and then confirmed by email are counted. Every figure is a total: we never publish anyone's email, postcode or individual answers, and any group of fewer than 10 people is shown as "fewer than 10".

      Constituencies are worked out inside our own systems from the first part of each signer's postcode (for example "NR2 2"), using the Office for National Statistics' lookup of every UK postcode to the July 2024 constituencies. Where that area crosses a boundary, its signers are shared between the constituencies in proportion. No postcode is sent to anyone else. Signers who live abroad are counted in the totals but not on the map.

      Each statement is shown with the dates it was asked. The live stream at the top shows the first name and town of people who signed in the last 24 hours, over and over; the number above it is the real count for today. Hexagon map layout by Open Innovations (CC BY-SA 4.0).
